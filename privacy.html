Your privacy.

A simple, transparent approach to your information.

Information on this website

The appointment enquiry form runs in your browser. It does not send, save or upload the information you enter. When you choose to copy the enquiry and send it through Instagram, you are sharing it directly through that service.

External services

Links to Instagram and Google Maps take you to third-party services with their own privacy policies. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts. This website does not use analytics or advertising cookies.

Appointment enquiries

Please do not include medical records or sensitive health information in the form. Contact the clinic directly for clinical questions. A request does not confirm an appointment.

Website imagery and information

Photography is illustrative, including an AI-generated hero image and licensed stock photography. It does not depict the clinic, its team or actual patients. Treatment information is introductory and is not individual medical advice.
