Information on this website

The appointment enquiry form runs in your browser. It does not send, save or upload the information you enter. When you choose to send an enquiry through your email app or Instagram, you are sharing it directly through that service.

External services

Links to email, Instagram and Google Maps take you to third-party services with their own privacy policies. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts. This website does not use analytics or advertising cookies.

Appointment enquiries

Please do not include medical records or sensitive health information in the form. Contact the clinic directly for clinical questions. A request does not confirm an appointment.

Website imagery and information

Photography is illustrative, supplied by the clinic for use on this website. It does not depict the clinic, its team or actual patients. Treatment information is introductory and is not individual medical advice.
