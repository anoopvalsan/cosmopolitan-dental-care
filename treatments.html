Ask us a question
What can I expect at my first visit?＋

Your first visit is an opportunity to share your concerns, discuss your goals and understand your options. Call +91 9496 801 445 to confirm what to bring and how much time to allow.

How do I book an appointment?＋

Call +91 9496 801 445 or prepare an enquiry on our contact page and send it by email or Instagram. The clinic will confirm availability; an enquiry is not a confirmed booking.

Which treatment is right for me?＋

Every smile is different. A clinical assessment is needed before recommending a treatment. Our treatment guides are an introduction, not a diagnosis.

Can I ask about the cost before treatment?＋

Yes. Please ask the clinic for an individual assessment and an estimate. Fees depend on your needs and the treatment plan.
